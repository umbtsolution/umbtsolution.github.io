This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Mëso Anglisht app on Android or iOS. Mëso Anglisht helps Albanian speakers learn English with vocabulary, grammar, quizzes, pronunciation practice, a translator, an AI Teacher and exam preparation.

There is no account to create and we do not sell personal information. Your learning progress is saved on your phone. The app is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. Some features need to send information to other services to work, such as the translator, the AI Teacher and speech recognition. This policy explains each of them.

Data controller: UMBT Solution
Contact: umbtsolution@gmail.com

1What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, we do not use your precise location, and we do not sell personal information. We do not keep recordings of your voice and we do not store the messages you write to the AI Teacher on our server.

2Your learning progress

Your progress is saved in the app's private storage on your device. This includes completed lessons and levels, points (XP), lives, your learning streak, test results, your AI Teacher chat history and the daily challenge.

This information is not uploaded to us and we have no way to see it. If you uninstall the app, everything it stored on your device is deleted, unless your phone's own system backup kept a copy.

3Microphone and speech recognition

Pronunciation exercises use the microphone only while you hold or tap the microphone button. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service: Google on Android and Apple on iOS. Depending on your device and settings, this service may process the audio on its own servers, under its own privacy policy.

The app receives only the recognized text, uses it to compare your words with the correct sentence, and does not save or send the audio anywhere.

Words and sentences are read aloud by your phone's text-to-speech engine. On Android the app prefers Google's natural online voices, so the English text being read aloud (not your voice) may be sent to Google to produce the audio.

4Translator

When you use the translator, the text you type or speak is sent to Google's translation service to get the translation. Please do not enter sensitive personal information in the translator. Google processes this text under its own privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5AI Teacher

The AI Teacher chats with you, explains grammar, corrects your writing, creates tests and runs role-play conversations. When you use it, the messages you send in that feature, with a short part of the earlier conversation for context, are sent to our server, which runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.). Our server forwards them to Groq, Inc., which runs the AI model that writes the answer.

To prevent abuse and to keep the service free for everyone, each request also includes a random identifier created by the app on your phone and is limited by your IP address for a short time. The identifier is not linked to your name, your advertising identifier or your account with any store.

Our server does not save the content of your messages. Groq processes requests under its own terms and privacy policy at https://groq.com/privacy-policy. Please do not share sensitive personal information with the AI Teacher.

AI answers can contain mistakes. If you see an incorrect or inappropriate answer, you can report it from the app. The report opens your email app with the answer, and nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email.

6Advertising

Mëso Anglisht shows ads from Google AdMob. The provider is Google Ireland Limited if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, and Google LLC everywhere else.

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow it);
  • your IP address and the approximate location that can be worked out from it;
  • basic device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • information about the ads you see and tap.

Google handles this data under its own policies, which you can read at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive your advertising identifier or IP address. We only see overall reports, such as how many ads were shown.

Some extras can be unlocked by watching a short video ad, for example time with the AI Teacher, extra lives in quizzes or a locked test level. Watching is always your choice. Full-screen ads between lessons are shown only at natural breaks and not more often than every few minutes.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's certified consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Info & More, then Privacy Settings. If you say no, you may still see ads that are not personalized, which use less data.

On iPhone, the app can use your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when Apple asks. You can change this later in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security and then Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, usually under Google and then Ads.

In some US states you have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. You can do this with the device settings described above and, where it appears, the Privacy Settings in the app.

7Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Firebase, a service of Google LLC, to understand how the app performs and to fix problems.

  • Firebase Analytics records general usage events, such as app opens, screens used and when an ad fails to load. It uses a random app instance identifier, basic device information and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We use these reports only in aggregate to improve the app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report if the app stops working. The report contains technical details about the error, such as which part of the app failed, the app version, your device model and operating system version, and a random identifier for this installation of the app.

Neither contains your progress, your messages to the AI Teacher or your voice. Google processes this data under the Firebase terms and Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

8Notifications and app updates

If you allow notifications, the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to receive occasional messages from us, for example about a new version or new lessons. For this, Firebase creates a random token for your device. We cannot see who you are from this token.

To tell you when a new version is available, the app checks Google Play's update service and reads the latest version number from Firebase Remote Config. No personal information is sent with these checks beyond the technical data needed to make them.

9Permissions

  • The microphone (and, on iPhone, speech recognition) is used only for pronunciation practice and voice input, as described in section 3.
  • Notifications are used for the messages described in section 8.
  • Internet access is used for ads, the translator, the AI Teacher, pronunciation audio on some devices, analytics, crash reports and update checks.
  • On Android, the app may also ask to run when the phone starts and to vibrate, so that notifications work correctly.

You can turn off any of these permissions in your device settings. The app will keep working, although the related feature may not.

10Legal basis for processing in the EEA and UK

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you:

  • Your progress is processed only on your own device so that the app can provide the features you use.
  • Translator, AI Teacher and speech recognition requests are processed to provide the feature you asked for.
  • Personalized ads are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app's Privacy Settings.
  • Non-personalized ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention, analytics and crash reports are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app free, secure and working well, or on your consent where the law requires it.

11International transfers

Google, Cloudflare and Groq may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. These companies use recognized safeguards for such transfers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

12How long data is kept

Your progress and chat history stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Our server does not keep your AI Teacher messages. Advertising and analytics data are kept by Google according to its retention policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention. Crash reports are deleted after 90 days.

13Security

Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read under the normal security rules of Android and iOS. Connections to our server and to the services we use are encrypted with HTTPS. We recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock.

14Children

Mëso Anglisht is meant for a general audience and is not aimed at children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help.

15Your rights

Because your progress lives only on your device, you are in full control of it, and we do not hold a copy. You can delete your AI Teacher chat history in the app at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent. For advertising and analytics data handled by Google, you can use the options in section 6 and the tools described in Google's privacy policy. For anything else, write to us at the address below and we will reply within the time the law allows.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

16Exam and visa information

The exam, visa and "first steps" guides in the app are general information and can change. They are not legal or immigration advice. Always check the official websites of the exam provider and of the relevant authority, such as gov.uk or travel.state.gov, before you apply.

17Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes or when the law requires it. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make important changes, we will mention them in the app or on its store page.
