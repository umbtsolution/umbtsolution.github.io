This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Pawsana app on Android or iOS.

Pawsana is built so that the information you enter about your pets stays on your phone. There is no account to create, we do not run a server that receives your pet data, and we do not sell personal information. The app is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. The ads are the only part of the app that processes personal data.

Data controller: UMBT Solution
Contact: umbtsolution@gmail.com

1What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location. We do not use analytics or crash reporting services. Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described in section 4, the app does not send information about you to anyone.

2Information you enter in the app

You can add details about your pets, such as their name, species, breed, sex, date of birth, weight, whether they are spayed or neutered, notes, photos, reminders and health records like vaccines, vet visits and treatments.

All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us and we have no way to see it.

When you add a photo from your camera or gallery, the app saves a copy in its private storage and uses it only for your pet's profile and pet card. The app opens your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo.

Reminders and birthday greetings are scheduled as local notifications by your phone's operating system. No server is involved.

If you delete a pet, its information and photo are removed from the app. If you uninstall the app, everything it stored on your device is deleted, unless you made a backup or your phone's own system backup kept a copy.

Please use the notes fields for information about your pets, not about other people.

3Backups, PDF exports and pet cards

The app creates files only when you ask it to. You can create a backup file containing your pet data and photos, a PDF of your pet's health record to send to a vet, or a pet card image to share with friends or on social media.

These files are passed to your phone's share menu and you decide where they go, for example a cloud drive, an email or a messaging app. From that point the file is handled by the service you chose, under its own privacy policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

When you restore a backup, the file is read on your device and replaces the data currently in the app. It is not sent to us.

4Advertising

Pawsana shows ads from Google AdMob. The provider is Google Ireland Limited if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, and Google LLC everywhere else.

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow it), your IP address and the approximate location that can be worked out from it, basic device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version, and information about the ads you see and tap.

Google handles this data under its own policies, which you can read at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive your advertising identifier or IP address. We only see overall reports, such as how many ads were shown.

The first aid screens never show ads.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's certified consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings, under Ad privacy options. If you say no, you may still see ads that are not personalized, which use less data.

On iPhone, the app can use your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when Apple asks. You can change this later in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security and then Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, usually under Google and then Ads.

In some US states you have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. You can do this with the device settings described above and, where it appears, the Ad privacy options in the app's Settings.

5Optional videos for premium content

Some extras can be unlocked by watching a short video: a behavior or training guide, which stays open while you read it, or a premium design for your pet card, which stays available while you are on the card screen. Watching is always your choice. The video is an ad from Google AdMob and is covered by section 4. The app does not keep any record of these unlocks.

First aid, the food safety checker, reminders, health records and health guides never require a video.

6Permissions

Notifications are used to show the reminders and birthday greetings you set up.

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a photo of your pet.

Internet access is used to load ads. Everything else in the app works offline.

On Android, the app also asks to run when the phone starts, so that your reminders are scheduled again after a restart.

You can turn off any of these permissions in your device settings. The app will keep working, although the related feature may not.

7Legal basis for processing in the EEA and UK

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, your pet data is processed only on your own device so that the app can provide the features you use. Personalized ads are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app's Settings, under Ad privacy options. Non-personalized ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app free and secure, or on your consent where the law requires it.

8International transfers

Google may process advertising data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google uses recognized safeguards for these transfers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Google's privacy policy has more details.

9How long data is kept

Your pet data, photos and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its own retention policy, available at https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention.

10Security

Your pet data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read under the normal security rules of Android and iOS. We recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock. Backup files you create are not encrypted, so please store them safely.

11Children

Pawsana is meant for a general audience and is not aimed at children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help.

12Your rights

Because your pet data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it. You can view, change, export or delete it in the app at any time, and we do not hold a copy.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent. For advertising data handled by Google, you can use the options in section 4 and the tools described in Google's privacy policy. For anything else, write to us at the address below and we will reply within the time the law allows.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

13Health information

The care guides, food checker and first aid content in Pawsana are general information and are not a substitute for advice from a veterinarian. In an emergency, contact your vet or the nearest emergency animal clinic straight away.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes or when the law requires it. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make important changes, we will mention them in the app or on its store page.

15Contact
